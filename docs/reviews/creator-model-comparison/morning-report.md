## Evidence delivered

- Twenty exact recorded invocation inputs are prepared: ten Main Creator and ten
  Edit Copilot, across five development threads from one creator. Source review
  is retained; narrower turn checklists and subjective scores await human review.
- Provider adapters, independent agent controls, versioned prices and usage,
  typed native Sol contracts, immutable manifests and bounded paid execution
  share the existing runtime and cost ledger. Production defaults remain Gemini.
- The source importer checks owner, job, creator event, invocation time window,
  complete unchanged input and snapshot hashes. It never substitutes the latest
  editor for the model's recorded pre-turn input.
- The new verifier compiles the returned bytes and uses real PostgreSQL draft
  services to commit, reopen, reject a stale write, undo, restore and reopen again.
  It refuses remote, production and paid-ledger databases. Synthetic scratch
  identities are removed after each check. Editor execution bases are explicitly
  reconstructed from recorded lanes plus owned jobs, separate from exact inputs.
- Independent holdout registration requires complete development owner/media
  exclusion history, exact inputs, source-media fingerprints and input-bound
  human obligations. Shared media across different creators is rejected. The
  admin-only inventory endpoint returns exposure metadata, including old revoked
  or expired entries, without request bodies or held-out identities.

## Findings that matter for selection

JSON validity, successful compilation and a saved draft are separate measurements.
A generic test initially rejected a valid clip-label edit because its synthetic job
lost the recorded cut-to-media link. The harness now reconstructs that association
and retains creator-edited provenance. The original live verdict is preserved;
corrected checks are separate receipts against the same returned bytes.

The compound real request distinguishes instruction following from plausible copy:
two candidates preserve all nine requested label strings, including duplicates and
Unicode, and add the exact last-clip title. One also changes previously static
labels to fade entrances. That extra change is visible as a preservation failure,
even though its JSON, compilation and persistence all succeed.

A separately versioned JSON serialization guard is available as an explicit opt-in
profile. It is not a reliable repair: a live guarded response still emitted invalid
function-call markup. Raw markup is not repaired into a successful result. Full
Sonnet native grammar remains blocked after provider rejection; capability-scoped
native contracts remain future work. The Gemini native smoke also failed; its
specific provider diagnostic was not retained, so no precise cause is claimed.
Sol native compatibility established in the earlier pilot is input-specific.

Lower-effort alignment controls produced usable edits that preserve the stacked
heights and unrelated right label. This single repeated task cannot establish that
lower effort is sufficient for broader planning or editing. Hook-tone candidates
return questions/options; subjective tone is unscored and draft saving does not
apply to those conversational responses.

The final timing cases also preserve the first/last clip exception and use a
three-second opener. Two usable opener edits differ in adjacent word overlap;
one additionally adds fade-out phases to eleven existing words. Exact intervals
and a word timeline chart are shown privately. These are state observations, not
a watched-render or human quality verdict. All three persistent-title strategies
carry the exact three literals and requested corners in structured fields.

## Remaining evidence

The full model-selection experiment is **not complete**. These are the exact gaps:

- Human blind preferences and final source-bound fulfillment judgments are pending.
  No assistant score has been substituted for a human assessment.
- Zero independent real holdouts are enrolled. All approved exports belong to an
  already exposed creator; development recaptures cannot become unseen holdouts.
  Complete owner and media exclusions must precede prospective enrollment.
- No candidate video has been rendered, watched or verified as an actual export.
  Scratch draft persistence and existing export-verifier tests do not supply that
  evidence. Physical-device sessions owned by other work were left alone.
- No fresh capture cohort, recurring purge, selected-model rollout or production
  deployment was activated. File-mode review cannot detect a later revocation;
  eligibility and derived-copy purge must run before further use. Private artifacts
  are indexed for conservative expiry/revocation cleanup.

The implementation is reviewable independently of a provider switch. Finish human
blind review first, then freeze agent-specific finalist profiles. Enroll a genuinely
independent eligible cohort with the existing sealed evaluator and price that
corpus before further paid work. Actual finalist export evidence remains a release
requirement; no additional budget is assumed.
