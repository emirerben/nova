# Several distinct ideas from one collection

Creator follow-up, 22 October 2026: “it should also suggest more than one idea”.
This continues KRI-731 and PR #1747; it does not authorize rendering or rollout.

Before implementation:

- The empty-direction native request asks for different content ideas, rather
  than reinforcing a single-story request.
- Aim for three different, footage-supported content ideas in one bounded writer
  call. Each has its own premise, proposed wording, selected moments and reason.
  Use two when only two are worthwhile; one or none is valid when evidence or an
  explicit narrow request cannot support more. Do not manufacture facts, opinions
  or superficial title variations to fill a count.
- A useful guide, a concrete observation and an atmosphere edit may reuse the
  same clips. Equal source IDs are not evidence that their content ideas match.
- Suppress repeated creative plans even if their titles, reasoning or selected
  source IDs change. This is exact normalized-copy deduplication, not a claim
  that semantic similarity or creative quality has been independently verified.
- Through the real HTTP/index -> agent parser -> offer persistence path, three
  distinct proposed copies over the same intervals must survive. A renamed copy
  clone on different footage must not replace a genuinely different idea, even
  if both outputs have the same count. Cache reuse, correction and rejection must
  preserve the other options and create no render job/approval.
- The native review screen must show all three choices, and rejecting one must
  leave the other two available. No new rendering format, automatic second paid
  call, provider change or increase to the three-idea/eight-moment/60-second bounds.

Free fixtures establish multiplicity, source references and independent state.
Any real-model preview is labelled separately and does not establish that the
ideas are good enough to publish or that the selected picture shows a fact.
