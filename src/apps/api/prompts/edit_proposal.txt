Editorial rules:
- Understand the whole set before choosing an order. Use both photos and videos when both exist,
  unless the creator's request is specifically about a subset of the media.
- The SOURCE VARIETY note above is guidance, not a hard schema requirement: aim for it on an
  ordinary request, but never force more distinct sources than the creator's request and the
  target duration actually support. With a typed mixed-media profile, stay target-capacity-aware:
  never force more sources than the target can fit at the profile's minimum per-kind holds.
- With at least 3 sources, 3-5 story beats is right for an ordinary guided_story/text_explainer
  with no strong grouping request of its own. $beat_count_note A CREATOR TEXT CONTRACT above
  overrides both this and the per-beat guidance below and sets the beat count. A fast_montage may
  leave story_beats empty; its actual edit is fast_cuts. A beat is a chapter that groups related
  media, not one beat per file. Prefer at most 4 media_ids per beat when practical, but when the
  creator's request groups more than 4 clips into one chapter, put them in one beat anyway -- an
  oversized beat is split automatically server-side, never rejected.
- Give each beat a distinct topic. Group coherent subjects such as food, architecture/town,
  coast/beaches, people, and sunset. Do not fabricate a topic.
- When the creator names a group by where or what is happening (such as "the beach clips" or
  "the part where I explain it to the camera"), use each row's `setting`, `activity`, and `speaks_to_camera`
  evidence to decide which media_ids belong to that beat -- `subject` alone may not mention it.
- A guided_story or text_explainer needs a visible intro; `thought` captions follow the rules below. A fast_montage
  is music-led: emit fast_cuts with exact source windows, a hook first, hard cuts only, and
  minimal generated text. Follow the timing note above: legacy plans use 0.8-1.2s cuts, while an

- `thought` is the caption shown on a chapter. There are two modes; decide which applies first.

  MODE B, NO ON-SCREEN TEXT SPECIFIED: the creator quoted nothing for the screen. Then `thought` is
