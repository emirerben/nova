SLIDE ZOOM (CURRENT POST shows `tilt_zoom_enabled: true`). set_slide_tilt also scales the picture:
- zoom is the size the slide should END at, 0.5 to 3 (1 = as shot, 2 = twice as close, 0.5 = half size with the backdrop around it). zoom_delta is an amount to ADD to the slide's current zoom (negative zooms out); the server adds it, so never add it yourself. Send one or the other, never both, and they combine with rotation_deg and backdrop in the same op.
- "zoom in" / "closer" with no number -> zoom_delta 0.5; "a bit" / "slightly" -> zoom_delta 0.25 (zoom_delta -0.25 for "a bit out"); "a lot" -> zoom_delta 1; "zoom out" -> zoom_delta -0.5. "zoom to 2x" / "twice as close" -> zoom 2. Zoom is only reported for a slide that is zoomed (`zoom=1.5` on its `slide N tilt` line); a slide without one is at 1.
- Zoom works on photos and videos and keeps the whole file, so "zoom in on slide 2" with no part of the picture named is set_slide_tilt zoom_delta, not a crop. Use set_slide_framing focus/zoom only when the creator names a part to keep ("zoom into the top of the photo").
- "fill the corners" / "no black corners" after a tilt of A degrees -> also send zoom at least cos(A) + 1.8*sin(A) rounded up: 3 degrees 1.1, 5 degrees 1.16, 8 degrees 1.25, 10 degrees 1.3, 15 degrees 1.45, 20 degrees 1.55, 30 degrees 1.8, 45 degrees 2. Use the slide's angle from CURRENT POST (or the one in this op).
- Zoom out below 1 shows the backdrop (blank by default); pair it with backdrop "blur" or "color" when the creator wants something behind the smaller picture.
- "straighten" now means rotation_deg 0 and keeps the zoom. reset:true removes the tilt AND the zoom: use it for "reset", "undo it", "put it back how it was". "reset the zoom" is zoom 1. Never combine reset with zoom, zoom_delta, rotation_deg or backdrop.
- Only the picture scales: text, drawings and pasted pictures stay put, so never edit them for a zoom.
- Examples: "zoom in on slide 2" -> {"op":"set_slide_tilt","slide_indexes":[1],"zoom_delta":0.5}; "tilt all slides 5 degrees and fill the corners" -> {"op":"set_slide_tilt","all":true,"rotation_deg":5,"zoom":1.16}; "make slide 3 zoom 2x" -> {"op":"set_slide_tilt","slide_indexes":[2],"zoom":2}; "slide 1 zoom out and put a blurred background" -> {"op":"set_slide_tilt","slide_indexes":[0],"zoom_delta":-0.5,"backdrop":"blur"}; "reset the zoom on every slide" -> {"op":"set_slide_tilt","all":true,"zoom":1}; "ikinci slaytı biraz yakınlaştır" -> {"op":"set_slide_tilt","slide_indexes":[1],"zoom_delta":0.25}.
