SPLIT SCREEN. Use only when split_screen_available is true. Otherwise explain that this edit cannot use split screen. Sections own simultaneous duration and layout; members remain ordinary independently editable media. Panel indexes run top to bottom: two panels are 0 top, 1 bottom; three are 0 top, 1 middle, 2 bottom. Read split_screen_sections and split member context first.
- create_split_screen {slot_indexes:[0,1],panel_count:2,duration_s:4} creates a section from consecutive ordinary slots, one source per panel. With one slot, duplicate it into independent panels. Optional panel_weights, gutter (0..0.1 of canvas height), background_color (#RRGGBB). Defaults use equal panels, no gutter and black. Select up to three source slots. The section replaces those slots; full-screen neighbors stay full screen. For a bounded portion of one clip, supply start_s and end_s in that clip's local playback seconds; full-screen material before and after remains intact. For the whole sequence use full_length:true with all its slot_indexes; every panel gets independent copies of the complete sequence with correct local offsets. Then replace or move specific panel sources with assign_split_panel.
- update_split_screen {section_id,duration_s?,panel_count?,panel_weights?,gutter?,background_color?} changes bounds/layout. Shortening trims coverage without changing playback speed. Extending leaves background gaps; ordinary duration edits extend media explicitly.
- assign_split_panel {section_id,slot_index,panel_index,offset_s:0,duplicate:false} moves an occurrence into a panel at a section-local offset. duplicate:true adds an independent copy. Assign top/middle/bottom from known source slots. Remove the old panel clip first when replacing it. Ordinary source, crop, audio, look and timing controls apply to panel slots. Members in a panel cannot overlap. Changing one member must not shift peers.
- swap_split_panels {section_id,first_panel:0,second_panel:1} swaps every member and its source clock intact.
- remove_split_screen {section_id,keep_panel:0} unstacks that panel into full-screen clips in offset order and discards other panels.
set_total_duration counts each section once and preserves playback rates while resizing coverage. trim_output_start removes the elapsed output window from all simultaneous panels. Use swap_split_panels for panel order.
