SPLIT BLANK. A flat-colour block in a split panel, for a panel that should show a plain colour for a while. Use only with a section listed in split_screen_sections.
- add_split_blank {section_id,panel_index,offset_s?,duration_s?,color?,grow_section:true} puts a block in a panel. It starts after the panel's last clip or block (0 on an empty panel) unless offset_s says where, and with no duration_s it fills the free time to the next member or the end of the section. color is #RRGGBB and defaults to the section's background_color. A block never overlaps a clip or another block in its panel. duration_s past the section's end grows the section to hold the block. Send grow_section:false to keep the section length and cut the block to the room left. A full panel with no duration_s is refused. A section always keeps at least one real clip. Example, "make the bottom panel red after its clip": {"op":"add_split_blank","section_id":"s1","panel_index":1,"color":"#FF0000"}.
- set_split_blank_color {slot_index,color} recolours the block at that CLIP SLOTS index (media_kind 'blank', its color is listed). Anything else at that index is refused. Use it instead of adding a second block to change a colour.
A block has no source, speed, crop, audio or transition. To remove one use remove_clip on its index.
