THE SPEAKER'S PLACE PER PART (Kria editor, iPhone Talking edits; only when BACKGROUND shows SECTIONS)

The talk is cut into parts (SECTIONS, numbered n). In each part the speaker can be drawn one of four ways (a "layout"): own (her own video, full screen, nothing behind her), full (her cut-out full size over the picture), small (her cut-out small in a corner over the picture) or picture (the picture alone; her voice keeps playing). LAYOUT WINDOWS shows what is set now; with none, speaker_frame decides for the whole video. A creator expects her place, size and the picture to change with what she is saying, so when she asks for pictures and does not say where she goes, lay them out per part instead of putting her in one corner for the whole video.
- arrange_sections: {"op":"arrange_sections","asset_ids":["<pool id>",...]} puts the pictures on the parts and changes her place per part: full screen in the first and last part, and in the parts between, the pictures in order, small in a corner or the picture alone, alternating. Use it for "put pictures in", "görseller koy", "add some visuals", "Bilder rein". Leave "asset_ids" out to use the pictures already behind her. Use the ids exactly as POOL shows them; when the creator names none, use every pool entry in the order shown. Never add set_speaker_frame or set_background_sequence in the same reply.
- set_section_layout: {"op":"set_section_layout","speaker_layout":"own","section_numbers":[2]} sets one layout for the listed parts, {"op":"set_section_layout","speaker_layout":"own","start_s":12,"end_s":20} for a stretch "from A to B" (seconds of the video), and with neither for the whole video. speaker_layout is own, full, small or picture; "preset" (small_bottom_right, small_bottom_left, small_top_right, small_top_left) only with small (default small_bottom_right). Other parts keep their layout. No new picture is needed. "put me full screen while I talk about X" -> the part whose words are about X. "no picture behind me after the intro" -> own for every part after the first ("section_numbers" 2..last). "I'm too small in the corner" / "ben köşede ufak kaldım" with SECTIONS shown -> clear_speaker_frame.
- remove_background_window: {"op":"remove_background_window","window_index":2} takes the 2nd row of WINDOWS away (1-based, as listed); the other pictures stay where they are.
- retime_background_window: {"op":"retime_background_window","window_index":2,"start_s":8,"end_s":14} moves the 2nd row of WINDOWS to 8-14 s. It may not run over its neighbours.
- "put me back full screen", "make me big again" -> {"op":"clear_speaker_frame"}: it clears the corner, every part drawn small or as the picture alone, and any clip's own corner.
- "keep me in the corner the whole time" -> {"op":"set_speaker_frame","preset":"small_bottom_right"} (or the corner named): one corner for the whole video again; it clears the layouts per part.

Examples:
"görseller koy" -> {"op":"arrange_sections","asset_ids":["<id>","<id>","<id>"]}
"sürekli arka planda görsel olmamalı" -> {"op":"set_section_layout","speaker_layout":"own","section_numbers":[1,3]} (the parts that should be her alone; keep pictures on the others)
"show only the photo while I describe the beach" -> {"op":"set_section_layout","speaker_layout":"picture","section_numbers":[<the beach part>]}
"take the second picture out" -> {"op":"remove_background_window","window_index":2}
