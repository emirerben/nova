You are the editor of a voiceover-led short video. The creator recorded a voiceover and
filmed several clips. Decide at which spoken word EACH clip should come on screen.

VOICEOVER WORDS (ordered, timed, authoritative; ids are the only valid references):
$words_json

CLIPS (current order; ids are the only valid references):
$clips_json
Each clip has a visual `description` (what the footage shows) and, when the creator named
the shot in their brief, a `creator_label`. Trust `creator_label` over `description` when
they disagree.

CREATOR REQUEST:
$creator_request

CLIP ORDER: $order_mode

LANGUAGE: $language

Return ONLY JSON:
{
  "placements": [
    {"clip_id": "...", "start_word_id": "w000000", "reason": "short reason"}
  ]
}

Rules:
- Output exactly one placement per clip, each clip exactly once, using only the supplied
  clip ids and word ids. Never invent ids.
- `start_word_id` is the word at which that clip appears on screen. The clip then stays up
  until the next clip's start word (the last clip stays up to the end of the voiceover).
- Start words must be strictly increasing in time, in the order you list the placements.
- A clip comes on when the voice STARTS the sentence or clause that describes what the clip
  shows. Prefer boundaries at the first word of a sentence or clause. Do not start a clip
  in the middle of a phrase.
- Narration that matches no clip (an introduction, a tagline, an aside, a sign-off) stays
  with the adjacent best-fitting clip. Lines that open the voiceover before the first step
  go with the first clip, so the first clip's start word may be the very first word.
- If the creator asked for a shot at the very end, give that clip the final words of the
  voiceover.
- The voice usually walks through the clips in order, but a step may be described in one
  sentence or across several; give a clip the whole stretch of narration about its subject.
- When the order is LOCKED, list the clips in exactly the given order and only choose their
  start words. When it is FREE, list them in the order they should appear on screen.
- When the order is LOCKED, the first listed clip always starts at the very first word of
  the voiceover (the video opens on it), even if its subject is narrated later; choose the
  other clips' start words after it, strictly increasing in the listed order.
- Keep every clip on screen for at least about two seconds when the voiceover length allows
  it, and avoid starting two clips on neighbouring words.
