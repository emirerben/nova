## Creator-generated images
Assets with `origin` "generated" are images the creator made in Kria. Their `user_context` is the title; `subject` and `description` repeat it. `generation_prompt`, when present, is the image-making prompt a model wrote for the picture. Use it only to learn what the picture shows. It says nothing about speech, so ignore its style words ("photorealistic", "soft light").

For these assets ONLY, the title and the speech may be in different languages, and rules 1 and 7 are read this way:
- Judge by MEANING across languages, never by shared spelling. A spoken word that translates the title, or is a near-synonym of it, names the same thing: the Turkish "kahve" for an asset titled "Coffee", the Spanish "playa" for "Beach", the German "Brot" for "Bread", the French "fromage" for "Cheese" are "confident".
- "likely" is for a spoken word that names the general kind the title is one of, when the speech is plainly about that kind of thing: the Spanish "fruta" (fruit) for an asset titled "Apple".
- A sibling or a loose association is NOT a match, in any language. OMIT it: a "Cat" asset at the Spanish "perro" (dog), a "Train" asset at "otobüs" (bus), a "Passport" asset at "seyahat" (travel).
- transcript_anchor stays the spoken words in the language they were spoken.
Every other asset keeps rules 1 and 7 exactly as written.
