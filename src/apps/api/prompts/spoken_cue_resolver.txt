A creator recorded a voiceover for a short video and asked for something to appear "when I
say" certain words (their cues). An automatic speech-to-text pass wrote down the voiceover.
An exact text match could not find these cues in it. For each cue, find where the speaker
says it, or answer null when they never say it.

VOICEOVER WORDS (in spoken order; ids are the only valid references):
$words_json

CUES (the creator's own spelling):
$cues_json

SPOKEN LANGUAGE: $language

Return ONLY JSON, one row per cue, in the order given:
{
  "resolutions": [
    {"cue_id": "...", "start_word_id": "w000000", "end_word_id": "w000002"},
    {"cue_id": "...", "start_word_id": null, "end_word_id": null}
  ]
}

Rules:
- Match by meaning, the way a listener would recognise the creator's words in the recording:
  - a number written as digits in one and as words in the other ("Null zu null" and "0 zu 0",
    "three" and "3", "on bir" and "11");
  - a part the speaker or the transcript dropped or added, such as a leading ordinal or
    article ("1. FC Nürnberg" said or written as "FC Nürnberg", "the Eiffel Tower" as
    "Eiffel Tower");
  - another inflection or form of the same words (case, plural, possessive, a verb form);
  - a transcription misspelling of the same spoken words ("Ampfiff" for "Anpfiff").
- The span is the words where the cue itself is said: start at its first spoken word and end
  at its last. Keep it as short as the cue (at most 8 words). Never stretch it over the
  surrounding sentence.
- Never pick a different moment: a word that merely sounds similar, a related topic, a
  synonym the speaker did not say, or the closest sentence is NOT the cue. If you are not
  sure the speaker says the cue, answer null.
- If the cue is said more than once, give its first occurrence.
- Answer every cue exactly once, using only the given cue ids and word ids. Never invent ids.
  Both ids are set, or both are null.
