# How making a video should work on iPhone, by edit type

Every video type follows the same five steps: **pick a format → add footage → chat with Kria → approve the plan → the video gets made**, either on your iPhone or in the cloud. The server decides which formats you see (`GET creation-threads/capabilities`). On iPhone-only accounts, it hides any format the phone cannot render yet.

**Montage** (`montage`). You pick Montage and add up to 50 clips. You can also add photos and videos ("Visuals") when this iPhone does the rendering. You tell Kria the vibe, for example "fast cuts, summer feel". Kria thinks, then shows a plan card. You tap "Create this video", and the video is made with music Kria picks, or with your own song. Your song can play as background music or in lip-sync mode, where the song stays where you filmed it. When the video is done you can open the editor or ask for another version.

**Narrated** (`narrated` → `narrated_planned`). Same steps, but the story is carried by a voice. If you recorded a voiceover, Kria uses it. If not, it uses the speech already in your footage. Before you approve, Kria may ask a speech check: clean up pauses and retakes, keep the original, or skip the check. Your answer goes in with your approval.

**Talking** (`talking_to_camera` → `subtitled`, or "talking head" with several clips). You add the video of you talking. When the multi-clip rollout is on, you can add up to 10 clips that show while you talk. Kria keeps your voice, adds captions, and can offer the same speech check as Narrated. Approving makes a subtitled talking video. On the phone path, extra clips become silent cutaways.

**Slider** (`slides`). Picking Slider leaves the chat and opens the Slider workspace. You add photos and videos, arrange slides, and add text, stickers and a look. The AI button opens a sheet where Kria edits or proposes an arrangement. Save keeps your changes. Export makes the file on your iPhone when it can. Otherwise the server renders the post ("Creating your post…"), and the app waits up to about 5 minutes. A Slider has no plan card.

**Planned video** (a content-plan item becomes a render). Every project is backed by a plan item. When you approve, the server locks that item and starts exactly one render job (`dispatch_item_render_for`). A duplicate approval only sees "already running". The job then goes either to the cloud (queued → processing → ready, or failed) or to your iPhone ("awaiting device" → preparing → rendering → ready on this iPhone → syncing → synced). If a phone render fails or is stopped, "Try again" asks for your approval again before a new export starts. If your iPhone never reports back, the server gives up after 24 hours and offers Try again. Opening a planned video from a link reuses its chat (`/for-editor`) and never starts a new render.
