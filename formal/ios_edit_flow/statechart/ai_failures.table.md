# AI steps reachable from an iOS video: failure table

`api/` = `src/apps/api/app/`. "Classes" are the `_runtime.py` errors as the caller sees them. Every `Agent.run` failure reaches the caller as a `TerminalError` subclass: `TerminalSchemaError`, `ProviderQuotaExceededError`, `ProviderOutcomeUnknownError` or `AiBudgetExceededError`. A refusal arrives as `TerminalError` with `__cause__=RefusalError`.
Outcome: **terminal** = fails visibly and you can act on it; **stuck** = no way forward, a loop, or a misleading dead end; **degraded** = finishes but weakens what you asked for.
